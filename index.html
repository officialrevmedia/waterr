Purification

Eleven steps between source and serve.

A Canadian certified process produced entirely on site, eliminating transportation emissions. Every stage is monitored and tested to exceed Canadian food and drug safety standards, finishing at pH 8.0 or higher.

01

AG Pre Filtration

Cartridge filtration removes particulate down to 20 microns, the first line of defence against sediment.

02

Activated Carbon

Removes chlorine, chloramine, solvents and lead while softening without salt, with automatic backwashing.

03

5 Micron Pre Filter

Captures fine particles, sediment and algae, protecting downstream membranes and ensuring clarity.

04

High Pressure R.O. Pump

Stainless steel pump raises pressure to 150 PSI for maximum membrane efficiency.

05

Reverse Osmosis

A 0.0002 micron membrane passes only pure water molecules, removing dissolved solids and heavy metals.

06

Protected Storage

FDA approved tanks preserve quality and guarantee supply through the highest demand periods.

07

Repressure System

A 20 GPM pump maintains optimal pressure and consistent flow throughout bottling.

08

Pressure Tank

A 30 gallon tank assures constant flow to the filler and stable pressure across the system.

09

Post Carbon Polish

Coconut shell carbon block polishing delivers an exceptionally smooth, neutral taste.

10

Ultraviolet Sterilization

Stainless steel UV eliminates up to 99.9% of bacteria with no chemicals added.

11

Ozone & Alkaline Finish

Optional ozone protects stored water; final enhancement delivers premium alkaline water at pH 8.0+.
